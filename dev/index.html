The Schwarzschild Metric

A black hole bends the paths of light around it. Photons passing close to the event horizon follow curved null geodesics, so the sky behind the hole is lensed, magnified, and mirrored into an Einstein ring. This text is live DOM — the canvas floats on top of it.

If the overlay is working, you can read these paragraphs clearly everywhere except where the hole distorts them. The accretion disk, photon ring, and starfield render on the transparent canvas above.

Drag the sliders on the right to tune the shader. Switch to Token mode and move the Token-level slider to grow and shrink the hole as it drifts across the page. Far from the hole the canvas is fully transparent, so this content stays crisp and (in Obsidian) clickable.

Gravitational time dilation freezes the inner disk; relativistic Doppler beaming brightens the approaching side. None of that should stop you from reading the parts of the page the hole isn't covering.
